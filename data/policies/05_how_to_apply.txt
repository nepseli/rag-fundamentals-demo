LUMORA LABS PTE LTD
LEAVE POLICY - PART 5: HOW TO APPLY FOR LEAVE
Policy version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026

1. PURPOSE
This section explains how to request leave, who approves it, and what happens to leave when employment ends.

2. WHERE TO APPLY
All leave requests are submitted through the Lumora HR portal. Each request must state the leave type, start and end date, and whether any day is a half day. Requests sent only by email or chat are not valid, except for same-day sick leave notifications to the line manager.

3. NOTICE PERIODS
Employees should submit annual leave requests with the following notice:
- 1 to 2 days of leave: at least 3 working days in advance
- 3 to 5 days of leave: at least 2 weeks in advance
- More than 5 days of leave: at least 4 weeks in advance
Shorter notice may be accepted when the manager agrees. Sick leave, compassionate leave and family emergency leave are exempt from notice periods.

4. WHO APPROVES
- Up to 5 consecutive working days: line manager
- More than 5 consecutive working days: line manager and department head
- Any leave between 15 December and 24 December: line manager and department head
- Unpaid leave: line manager and HR
- Work-from-anywhere weeks: line manager, IT security and HR

5. APPROVAL TIMELINE
Managers should respond to leave requests within 3 working days. If a request has had no response after 5 working days, HR will follow up with the manager. A request is not approved until its status in the HR portal shows Approved. Employees should not book non-refundable travel before approval.

6. CONFLICTING REQUESTS
If several employees in the same team request the same dates and the team cannot operate with that many people absent, the manager decides based on the order in which requests were submitted, and then on business needs. Managers should explain the reason if a request is declined.

7. CANCELLING OR CHANGING LEAVE
Employees can cancel or change approved leave in the HR portal up to 2 working days before the start date. Later changes require manager approval. Leave days are returned to the balance when leave is cancelled.

8. RECALL FROM LEAVE
The company may only recall an employee from approved leave in exceptional business circumstances and with the agreement of a department head. Employees who are recalled will have the unused days restored and will be reimbursed for reasonable, documented, non-refundable costs.

9. LEAVE WHEN EMPLOYMENT ENDS
When an employee resigns or is terminated, unused annual leave for the year is calculated on a pro-rated basis for the complete months worked. Unused pro-rated days are paid out at the employee's daily salary rate. If the employee has taken more leave than they have earned, the excess days are deducted from the final salary. Notice periods cannot be offset by using annual leave unless HR and the manager agree.

10. LEAVE BALANCE ENQUIRIES
Employees can see their current balance for each leave type in the HR portal. For corrections or questions, contact the HR team at the address given in the portal's help section.
