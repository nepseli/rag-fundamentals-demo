LUMORA LABS PTE LTD
LEAVE POLICY - PART 1: ANNUAL LEAVE
Policy version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026

1. PURPOSE
Annual leave gives employees time to rest and recharge. This section explains how much annual leave each employee receives, how it accrues, and what happens to unused days.

2. ELIGIBILITY
All full-time employees of Lumora Labs are entitled to paid annual leave from their first day of employment. Part-time employees receive annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Interns and contractors are not covered by this policy.

3. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
The number of paid annual leave days per calendar year depends on completed years of service:
- Less than 2 years of service: 14 days per year
- 2 years but less than 5 years of service: 16 days per year
- 5 years but less than 8 years of service: 18 days per year
- 8 years of service or more: 21 days per year
Years of service are counted from the employee's start date. The higher entitlement applies from the 1st of January of the year in which the employee completes the relevant service milestone.

4. PRO-RATING FOR NEW JOINERS AND LEAVERS
Employees who join part-way through the year receive annual leave pro-rated by the number of complete months remaining in that calendar year. For example, an employee with an entitlement of 14 days who joins on 1 July receives 7 days for that year. Fractions are rounded up to the nearest half day. Employees who leave part-way through the year are entitled to a pro-rated amount based on the complete months worked in their final year.

5. CARRY-OVER OF UNUSED LEAVE
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of the following year. Any carried-over days not used by 31 March are forfeited and will not be paid out. Days above the 5-day limit that are unused at 31 December are forfeited unless the employee's manager and HR have agreed in writing that business needs prevented the employee from taking leave.

6. ENCASHMENT
Unused annual leave cannot be exchanged for cash during employment. The only exception is on resignation or termination, where unused pro-rated leave is paid out as described in Part 5 of this policy (How to Apply).

7. YEAR-END BLACKOUT PERIOD
To protect delivery of year-end releases, annual leave requests that fall between 15 December and 24 December require approval from the department head in addition to the line manager. The company shutdown from 25 December to 31 December does not use any annual leave days.

8. HALF-DAY LEAVE
Annual leave may be taken in half-day units, either morning (9:00 to 13:00) or afternoon (14:00 to 18:00). Requests shorter than a half day are not recorded as leave.

9. LEAVE DURING PROBATION
Employees on probation may take annual leave that they have accrued. Leave requests of more than 5 consecutive working days during probation need approval from HR.
