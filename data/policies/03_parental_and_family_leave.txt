LUMORA LABS PTE LTD
LEAVE POLICY - PART 3: PARENTAL AND FAMILY LEAVE
Policy version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026

1. PURPOSE
This section covers leave that supports employees at important family moments: the arrival of a child, caring for children, and bereavement.

2. MATERNITY LEAVE
Female employees who give birth are entitled to 16 weeks of paid maternity leave. Of these, up to 4 weeks may be taken before the expected delivery date. The remaining weeks follow the birth. Employees must have completed at least 3 months of service to qualify for fully paid maternity leave. Employees with less than 3 months of service receive maternity leave at the statutory rate.

3. PATERNITY LEAVE
Employees whose partner gives birth are entitled to 4 weeks of paid paternity leave. Paternity leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth. The leave may be taken in one block or in up to two blocks of at least one week each. The employee must have completed at least 3 months of service to qualify.

4. SHARED PARENTAL LEAVE
A birth mother may transfer up to 4 of her 16 weeks of maternity leave to her partner as shared parental leave, provided the partner is also a Lumora Labs employee or can show employer confirmation. The transfer must be requested in writing at least 4 weeks before the maternity leave starts. Shared parental leave is paid at the same rate as the paternity leave.

5. ADOPTION LEAVE
Employees who adopt a child under 12 months of age are entitled to 12 weeks of paid adoption leave, starting from the date the child is placed with the family. Only one parent per adoption may take adoption leave; the other parent may take paternity leave.

6. CHILDCARE LEAVE
Employees with a child under 7 years of age are entitled to 6 days of paid childcare leave per calendar year. Childcare leave may be used for school events, medical visits, or when the child is unwell. Childcare leave is taken per employee. If both parents work at Lumora Labs, each is entitled to 6 days. Unused childcare leave does not carry over.

7. EXTENDED CHILDCARE LEAVE
Employees with a child between 7 and 12 years of age are entitled to 2 days of paid extended childcare leave per calendar year, under the same conditions as childcare leave.

8. COMPASSIONATE LEAVE
Employees are entitled to paid compassionate leave on the death of a family member:
- Spouse, child, parent: 5 working days
- Sibling, grandparent, parent-in-law: 3 working days
- Other relatives or close friends: 1 working day, at the manager's discretion
Compassionate leave may be extended using annual leave or unpaid leave if the employee needs to travel overseas.

9. FAMILY EMERGENCY LEAVE
Employees may take up to 2 days of paid family emergency leave per year for urgent situations involving immediate family, such as a sudden hospitalisation or a home emergency. Employees should inform their manager as soon as possible and provide a short explanation afterwards.
