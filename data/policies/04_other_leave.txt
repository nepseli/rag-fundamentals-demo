LUMORA LABS PTE LTD
LEAVE POLICY - PART 4: OTHER TYPES OF LEAVE
Policy version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026

1. PURPOSE
This section covers leave types that do not fall under annual, sick, or family leave.

2. UNPAID LEAVE
Employees who have completed at least 1 year of service may apply for unpaid leave of up to 30 calendar days per year. Unpaid leave requests must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance and need approval from the line manager and HR. During unpaid leave, salary is not paid and the employee's service record continues. Medical benefits remain active for the first 30 days. Unpaid leave of more than 30 days is treated as a career break and needs approval from the department head and HR director.

3. STUDY AND EXAM LEAVE
Employees pursuing a job-related course or professional qualification may take up to 5 days of paid study leave per calendar year. Paid exam leave is granted for the day of each exam, up to a maximum of 5 exam days. Courses must be approved in advance by the line manager. Employees who leave within 12 months after the company has paid for their course fees must repay the fees on a pro-rated basis.

4. VOLUNTEER DAY
Every employee receives 1 paid volunteer day per calendar year to spend with a registered charity or community organisation. The employee needs to share a short description of the activity with HR after the event. The volunteer day cannot be carried over or combined with another employee's day.

5. NATIONAL SERVICE LEAVE
Employees who are required to attend reservist or national service training are granted paid national service leave for the duration of the training, up to 40 days per calendar year. The employee should provide the call-up notice to HR as soon as it is received. The company will not require employees to work during national service leave.

6. WORK-FROM-ANYWHERE WEEKS
Employees who have completed 1 year of service may work from a different country for up to 4 weeks per calendar year, subject to approval. Work-from-anywhere weeks are not leave; employees remain at work and are paid normally. They require approval from the line manager, IT security, and HR, and employees are responsible for confirming that they have the right to work in the destination country. Requests should be submitted at least 6 weeks in advance.

7. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
Lumora Labs observes all Singapore public holidays. When a public holiday falls on a Sunday, the following Monday is given as a holiday in lieu, in line with Singapore regulations. When a public holiday falls on a Saturday, no replacement day is given, as the standard work week is Monday to Friday. Public holidays that fall during annual leave are not deducted from the annual leave balance.

8. BIRTHDAY LEAVE
Each employee receives 1 paid birthday leave day per calendar year. The day must be taken within the calendar month of the employee's birthday. Birthday leave cannot be carried over or exchanged for cash.

9. JURY AND COURT ATTENDANCE
Employees summoned for jury duty or required to attend court as a witness receive paid leave for the days required. A copy of the summons should be provided to HR.
