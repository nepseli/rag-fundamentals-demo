LUMORA LABS PTE LTD
LEAVE POLICY - PART 2: SICK AND MEDICAL LEAVE
Policy version 3.2, effective 1 January 2026

1. PURPOSE
Sick and medical leave protects employees who are unwell or recovering from illness or injury. It is separate from annual leave and does not reduce the annual leave balance.

2. OUTPATIENT SICK LEAVE
Every full-time employee receives 14 days of paid outpatient sick leave per calendar year. Outpatient sick leave covers illness treated at a clinic or at home, including recovery days recommended by a doctor. Unused outpatient sick leave does not carry over to the next year.

3. HOSPITALISATION LEAVE
In addition to outpatient sick leave, employees receive up to 60 days of paid hospitalisation leave per calendar year. Hospitalisation leave applies when the employee is admitted to a hospital, and to the recovery period that the treating doctor recommends after discharge. The 14 days of outpatient sick leave are counted first; hospitalisation leave starts only for periods that involve an admission.

4. MEDICAL CERTIFICATE REQUIREMENTS
A medical certificate (MC) from a registered doctor is required when sick leave lasts more than 2 consecutive working days. For absences of 1 or 2 consecutive working days, no certificate is needed, but the employee must inform their manager before the start of the working day, or as soon as reasonably possible. An MC is always required if sick leave is taken immediately before or after a public holiday or a weekend, or after the employee has taken more than 5 uncertified sick days in a calendar year.

5. HOW TO REPORT SICK LEAVE
Employees should message their line manager before 9:30 on the first day of absence. For absences that continue beyond one day, the employee should update their manager each day unless the MC covers the whole absence. The MC should be uploaded to the HR system within 3 working days of returning to work.

6. MENTAL HEALTH DAYS
Each employee may take up to 3 mental health days per calendar year. Mental health days are deducted from the 14 days of outpatient sick leave. No medical certificate is needed for these days and employees do not have to give a reason beyond stating that they are taking a mental health day.

7. LONG-TERM ILLNESS
If an employee's absence is expected to exceed the 60-day hospitalisation limit, HR will meet the employee to discuss options, which may include unpaid medical leave under Part 4, a phased return to work, or adjusted duties. Employment is not terminated solely because of illness while a medical leave arrangement is in place.

8. CARING FOR A FAMILY MEMBER
Employees cannot use their own sick leave to care for a sick family member. Care for a sick child is covered by childcare leave under Part 3, and urgent family situations may be covered by compassionate leave or annual leave.

9. FALSE CLAIMS
Submitting a false medical certificate or using sick leave for a purpose other than genuine illness is a disciplinary matter and may result in action up to termination of employment.
